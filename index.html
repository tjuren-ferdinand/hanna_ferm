About Hanna

The story behind the music

Hanna Ferm has established herself as one of Scandinavia's most undeniable voices. With a vocal range that defies limits and a rare musical intuition, she has made the journey from major television stages to becoming a critically acclaimed singer-songwriter on her own terms.

After massive success in national competitions and millions of streams, Hanna entered a new, more intimate chapter of her career. Transitioning into a dreamy and cinematic pop landscape, she places honesty and vulnerability at the core of her art.

Her recent work, including the celebrated EP 'om vi försvann' and the hit single 'ett minne i taget', showcases a sophisticated evolution into an artist who commands her creative process from the first note to the final visual detail.

Hanna Ferm is not just a performer; she is a visionary shaping the future of the modern Nordic sound.
